Good to know

Questions, answered.

Can everyone see the reports?

Public reports are intended to help people understand local issues and avoid duplicates. Private account information and restricted government functions are separate.

Does Civic AI decide what government must do?

No. Civic AI can offer analysis and suggestions. Government officials are responsible for reviewing reports and deciding what action to take.

Can anyone open the Government Portal?

Anyone can view the portal preview, but access to official account functions is restricted to authorised government accounts.
